# OnPath 0.18.0 – Multi-day tours, saved places and a tidier inside

**Multi-day tours**
- **Plan a tour over several days** (in the browser planner, synced to the phone): nights along the route split it into days; the plan sheet shows the whole tour with its day borders in the profile and one row per day.
- **„Ride day n"** follows one day as a plan of its own; changes go back into the whole tour.
- **The tour's maps come along:** the map areas along the whole route (10 km wide) load by themselves, on Wi-Fi.

**Planning**
- **Holding the map next to your plan:** the app measures both ways on the real paths – a stop on the way or a new destination – and takes the shorter one. It only asks („Neues Ziel / Einfügen") when both are almost the same. Holding directly on the line still adds a stop there.
- **Holding a place on the map** (restaurant, bus stop …): its menu shows only the row that fits; both only when it is really in between.
- **Saved places:** „Zuhause" on top of the empty search, favourites with „★ Gemerkt" in a place's menu – now also synced with the browser planner.
- „Zuletzt gesucht" keeps 20 places.

**Map**
- **Place names only from zoom 17,** always next to their symbol – no more lone names that looked like street names. Below that, symbols only.
- **A tap on a place** shows its details everywhere, also with a menu open or on a place that is a stop. The details show the same symbol as the map.
- **New map version:** after this update, a new map version downloads only what changed (often a few hundred KB instead of 20 MB per area).

**My routes**
- **A recorded tour looks back on itself:** big values (distance · climb · average speed), the picture with the height profile, moving time and breaks, faster or slower than planned, highlights.
- **Average speed** in the list of done tours: „32,8 km · ↑ 940 m · 10,3 km/h".
- Swipe through the large overviews („3 von 27").

**Recording**
- **Back during a recording stays in the app** – no more „App verlassen?" question.

**Inside**
- A big cleanup of the app's inside (nothing you see changed): clearer parts, many new tests, a crash hunt with 30 000 random taps without a crash.
